How the classifications work. “Authoritative” comes from the ArcGIS item contentStatus. Living Atlas is detected from the item’s Living Atlas designation/keywords. Subscriber content is detected from subscription-required keywords; Premium is the subset marked as requiring credits. A token-required response is reported as Restricted / sign-in required, not as a broken layer. A browser network/CORS failure is shown as Not responding / blocked because a client-only page cannot always distinguish those cases. Private maps and private item metadata cannot be fully audited anonymously.
